About

Bryanne Biegel, CPA, MAcc

Founder

Public accounting background spanning audit, financial reporting, and bookkeeping. Built deep AI expertise independently. Now bridging both — helping bookkeeping and accounting firms and small businesses become genuinely AI-empowered.

Bryanne brings accounting and consulting experience across some of the most varied and demanding business environments — small owner-operated businesses, oil and gas litigation, valuations, agriculture, logging, non-profits, and Indigenous organizations.

That breadth means she understands how different every business actually is — and why cookie-cutter AI advice doesn't work. She self-taught AI and built AI-Empowered Solutions to bridge the gap between real-world business operations and what AI can actually do for them.
